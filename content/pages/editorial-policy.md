## Our commitments

1. **Accuracy over completeness.** We would rather leave a gap than publish something invented or unverified.
2. **Transparency about uncertainty.** We label information that is approximate, unverified or awaiting confirmation.
3. **Respect.** We write about religious beliefs respectfully and accurately, without mockery and without exaggeration.
4. **Independence.** We are not affiliated with the temple trust or any government body, and we say so clearly.
5. **Usefulness.** Every page should help a real visitor or reader.

## How information is researched

We prioritise sources in this order:

1. **Official and primary sources** — the temple trust's own publications; government, district, railway and airport authorities; court records.
2. **Reputable publications** — established newspapers, academic works and recognised reference works.
3. **Careful comparison** — for practical information such as distances, we compare several sources and publish ranges rather than false precision.

We do not rely on low-quality websites merely because they rank highly in search, and we do not copy other websites' text. See [how we research](/how-we-research/) for a detailed description.

## History, tradition and belief

Religious sites combine different kinds of knowledge. We keep them distinct:

- **Documented history** — events supported by records or reputable reporting. We give dates where they are reliable.
- **Traditional accounts** — stories passed down in the community. We introduce them with phrases such as "according to local tradition".
- **Religious beliefs and interpretations** — what devotees believe about the deity, worship and its effects. We describe these as beliefs, not as scientific or proven facts.
- **General devotional information** — practices common across India, as distinct from what happens at Shingnapur specifically.

Pages use labelled boxes ("Tradition & belief", "Needs verification") to make these distinctions visible.

## Claims we will not make

- That any ritual, mantra, pooja or gemstone **guarantees** a particular result.
- Medical, financial or legal advice based on astrology.
- That this website is official, or that we speak for the temple.
- Invented timings, fees, phone numbers, schedules, prices, statistics, quotes, reviews or testimonials.

## Keeping information current

Temple timings, festival arrangements, transport and accommodation change. We:

- Show a **"Last updated"** date on every article, reflecting when the content was last revised.
- Show **verification labels** on individual facts that change.
- Do not claim information was recently verified unless it actually was.
- Review time-sensitive pages regularly and after major festivals.

## Corrections

If you find an error, please [contact us](/contact/) with the page address and, ideally, a source. We will:

1. Review the report against the best available sources.
2. Correct the page promptly if we agree it is wrong.
3. Update the "Last updated" date.
4. For significant corrections, add a short note explaining what changed.

## Images

We use only original photographs or images whose licence we have confirmed, with credits. Illustrations on this site are original.

## Advertising and affiliate links

If the site carries advertising, ads will be clearly labelled and placed so they do not interfere with reading or navigation. If we use affiliate links, we will disclose them clearly on the page. Neither advertising nor affiliate relationships influence our content or recommendations.

## Artificial intelligence and tools

We may use software tools, including AI tools, to help with drafting, checking and organising content. All published content is reviewed against sources before publication, and responsibility for accuracy rests with the editorial team.
