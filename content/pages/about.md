## What this website is

ShaniShingnapurTemple.com is an **independent informational guide** to Shani Shingnapur Temple and the surrounding pilgrimage region of Maharashtra. Our aim is to be the most useful, accurate and honest single resource for people planning a visit or wanting to understand the temple and its traditions.

We cover:

- The [temple](/temple/) — its history, timings, darshan and worship
- [Shani Dev](/shani-dev/) — beliefs, mantras and observances, explained respectfully
- [Travel](/travel/) — how to get there from each major city, where to stay and practical tips
- [Pilgrimage itineraries](/pilgrimage/) and [nearby places](/nearby-places/)
- [Festivals](/festivals/), with a calculated [calendar](/festivals/festival-calendar/)

## What this website is not

:::warning Independent website

:::

- We **do not** sell darshan, pooja, prasad or accommodation bookings.
- We **do not** accept donations on behalf of the temple.
- We **cannot** answer questions on behalf of the temple trust.

For official information, please use the trust's own website: [shanidev.com]({{temple.officialWebsite}}).

## Who we are

This site is written and maintained by the **{{site.editorialTeam}}** — a small independent team interested in Maharashtra's pilgrimage heritage and in practical, trustworthy travel information. We do not claim religious authority or professional credentials in astrology, history or theology. Where a topic calls for such expertise, we rely on published sources and say so.

## How we work

- We separate **documented facts**, **traditional accounts** and **religious beliefs**, and label them.
- We **do not invent** timings, fees, phone numbers, schedules or prices. When we don't know something, we say so.
- We show **verification labels** on facts that change, such as timings.
- We **update pages** when things change and show the date of the last update.

Read our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) and [how we research](/how-we-research/) for details.

## How the site is funded

The site may in future carry clearly labelled advertising and, where genuinely useful, clearly disclosed affiliate links (for example to hotel bookings). Advertising and affiliate relationships will never decide what we write or recommend.
