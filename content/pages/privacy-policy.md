This policy explains what information ShaniShingnapurTemple.com ("we") collects, why, and what choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

## Information you give us

If you use our [contact form](/contact/), we receive your **name, email address, subject and message**. We use this only to read and, where appropriate, respond to your message, and to improve the website (for example, by acting on a correction). Messages are delivered to us through a third-party form-handling or messaging service; we do not sell or share your details for marketing.

We keep messages only as long as needed for these purposes.

## Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information — such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested — in server logs, for security and reliability.

### Analytics

We may use **Google Analytics** to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are popular). If enabled, it uses cookies or similar technologies. We configure it to limit data collection and do not use it to identify individuals.

### Advertising

If we display advertising (for example, through **Google AdSense**), the advertising provider may use cookies to show and measure ads, which may include personalised advertising depending on your settings and location. You can learn more and control personalised ads through [Google's ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

Where the law requires consent for such cookies in your location, we will ask for it before they are set.

## Your choices

- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- You can use browser extensions or settings to opt out of analytics.
- You can contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you from contact-form messages, or to ask us to delete it.

## Children

This website is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Links to other websites

We link to other websites, including the temple trust and government sites. Their privacy practices are their own.

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date below shows when it was last changed.
