## The route at a glance

This circuit links five of Maharashtra's best-known pilgrimage places in a sensible west-to-east line, which avoids doubling back:

**Mumbai → Nashik & Trimbakeshwar → Shirdi → Shani Shingnapur → Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Grishneshwar & Ellora)**

| Leg | Approx. road distance | Typical drive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mumbai → Nashik | About 165–175 km | About 3.5–4.5 hours |
| Nashik ↔ Trimbakeshwar | About 28 km each way | About 45 min–1 hour |

## Suggested four-day plan

### Day 1 — Nashik and Trimbakeshwar

- Drive or take a train from Mumbai to Nashik early.
- Visit **Trimbakeshwar** Jyotirlinga (queues can be long; Mondays and the month of Shravan are busiest).
- Evening at **Ramkund** and **Panchavati** in Nashik.
- Overnight in Nashik.

See [Nashik](/nearby-places/nashik/) and [Trimbakeshwar](/nearby-places/trimbakeshwar/).

### Day 2 — Shirdi

- Drive to Shirdi (about 2–2.5 hours).
- Sai Baba darshan; Dwarkamai, Chavadi and Gurusthan.
- Overnight in Shirdi.

See [Shirdi](/nearby-places/shirdi/).

### Day 3 — Shani Shingnapur, then on to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

- Leave Shirdi at sunrise for Shingnapur.
- Darshan and a walk through the village.
- Continue to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (about 2 hours).
- Overnight in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

### Day 4 — Grishneshwar and Ellora

- **Grishneshwar** Jyotirlinga at Verul.
- The **Ellora Caves** next door (closed on Tuesdays).
- Fly or take a train home from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

See [Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar](/nearby-places/chhatrapati-sambhajinagar/).

## Shorter and longer versions

- **Three days:** Drop Day 1 and start in Shirdi, or drop Day 4 and return from Shingnapur via Ahilyanagar and Pune.
- **Five days or more:** Add the Ajanta Caves (closed on Mondays), a further drive from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, or spend longer at each site.

## Timing the circuit

| Consider | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Avoid Saturday at Shingnapur | Heaviest crowds of the week |
| Avoid Thursday at Shirdi | Sai Baba's special day; very busy |
| Mondays and Shravan at Shiva temples | Trimbakeshwar and Grishneshwar are busiest |
| Tuesday | Ellora Caves closed |
| Kumbh Mela years | Nashik–Trimbakeshwar sees enormous crowds during the Simhastha Kumbh |

## Getting around

A car with a driver for the whole circuit is by far the easiest option. Public transport is possible — trains to Nashik Road and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, and MSRTC buses between the towns — but allow more time.

## Before you go

- Check each temple's current timings and any dress rules for inner sanctums.
- Book accommodation ahead for weekends and festivals.
- Read [Shani temples in Maharashtra](/blog/shani-temples-in-maharashtra/) if you want to add more Shani shrines.
