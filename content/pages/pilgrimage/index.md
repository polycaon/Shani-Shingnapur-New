## A region of pilgrimage

Shani Shingnapur sits in one of India's most visited pilgrimage regions. Within a few hours' drive are Shirdi, the Jyotirlingas of Trimbakeshwar and Grishneshwar, and the holy city of Nashik on the Godavari. Many pilgrims plan routes that link several of these places.

## Planning principles

- **Fewer places, done well.** Queues and travel always take longer than expected.
- **Mind the calendar.** Saturdays and festival days are crowded at Shingnapur; Thursdays are busy at Shirdi; Mondays and Shravan are busy at Shiva temples such as Trimbakeshwar and Grishneshwar.
- **Start early every day.** It is cooler and quieter.
- **Keep one flexible slot** for rest or a delayed darshan.
