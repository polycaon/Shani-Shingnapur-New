## Why two days?

Two days let you:

- Take darshan at both temples at quieter times of day
- Attend an aarti in Shirdi without rushing
- Cope with long queues without missing anything
- Travel comfortably with children or elders
- Add an extra stop

## Itinerary for visitors already in the region

### Day 1 — Shirdi

| Time (approx.) | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | Arrive in Shirdi; check in |
| Late morning | Sai Baba Samadhi Mandir darshan |
| Afternoon | Dwarkamai, Chavadi, Gurusthan and Lendi Baug; lunch |
| Evening | Evening aarti or a quiet walk; early night |

### Day 2 — Shani Shingnapur

| Time (approx.) | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Around sunrise | Leave Shirdi for Shingnapur |
| Early–mid morning | Darshan; walk through the village |
| Late morning | Breakfast or early lunch |
| Afternoon | Return to Shirdi, or continue to Ahilyanagar, Pune or Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar |

## Itinerary for travellers from Mumbai

- **Day 1:** Leave Mumbai before dawn; drive or take a train to Shirdi; afternoon and evening in Shirdi; overnight in Shirdi.
- **Day 2:** Early drive to Shingnapur (about 1.5–2 hours); darshan; return to Mumbai via Ahilyanagar and Pune, or back via Shirdi and Nashik.

See [from Mumbai](/travel/from-mumbai/).

## Itinerary for travellers from Pune

- **Day 1:** Drive via Ahilyanagar to Shingnapur (about 3.5–4.5 hours); afternoon darshan; continue to Shirdi (about 1.5–2 hours); overnight in Shirdi.
- **Day 2:** Morning darshan in Shirdi; return to Pune (via Sangamner and Alephata, or via Ahilyanagar).

See [from Pune](/travel/from-pune/).

## Where to stay

Shirdi has the widest choice; the village of Shingnapur has trust-run and private lodging for those who want the earliest possible darshan. See [accommodation](/travel/accommodation/).
