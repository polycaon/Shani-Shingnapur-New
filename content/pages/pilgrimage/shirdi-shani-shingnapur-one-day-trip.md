## Before you start

This itinerary assumes you are **staying in Shirdi** and travelling to Shingnapur by **taxi or hired car**. If you are travelling by bus, allow significantly more time and consider the [two-day plan](/pilgrimage/shirdi-shani-shingnapur-two-day-itinerary/).

All times below are **illustrative**. Check the current Shirdi aarti and darshan schedule on [sai.org.in](https://sai.org.in/), and see [Shani Shingnapur timings](/temple/timings/).

## Plan A: Shingnapur in the morning (recommended for weekdays)

| Time (approx.) | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Around sunrise | Leave Shirdi by taxi |
| Early–mid morning | Reach Shingnapur; darshan at the platform |
| Mid-morning | Walk through the village; tea or breakfast |
| Late morning | Drive back to Shirdi |
| Early afternoon | Lunch and rest in Shirdi |
| Afternoon–evening | Sai Baba darshan; visit Dwarkamai, Chavadi and Gurusthan |
| Evening | Optional evening aarti in Shirdi |

**Why this works:** you reach Shingnapur before the day-trippers and the heat, and you have the rest of the day for Shirdi, where queues are more variable.

## Plan B: Shirdi in the morning, Shingnapur in the afternoon

| Time (approx.) | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Early morning | Sai Baba darshan (or morning aarti, if you have arranged it) |
| Mid-morning | Breakfast; visit Dwarkamai and Chavadi |
| Late morning | Leave for Shingnapur |
| Early afternoon | Darshan at Shingnapur; lunch |
| Afternoon | Return to Shirdi |
| Evening | Rest, shopping, or evening aarti |

**Trade-off:** you will be at Shingnapur during the hottest part of the day. Better in the cooler months (October–February).

## Adapting for Saturdays

If you must go on a Saturday:

- Leave even earlier and expect longer queues and traffic at Shingnapur.
- Keep your Shirdi plans flexible — you may return later than planned.
- Consider switching to the [two-day plan](/pilgrimage/shirdi-shani-shingnapur-two-day-itinerary/).

## What to carry

Water, sun protection, modest clothing, slip-on footwear, small change and UPI, any medicines, and your driver's phone number. See [travel tips](/travel/travel-tips/).

## Booking the taxi

Agree in advance:

- Round trip with waiting time at Shingnapur
- Any extra stops
- Tolls and parking
- Total price (in writing)
