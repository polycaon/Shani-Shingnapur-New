:::warning Before you write
We are an **independent website**, not the temple trust. We cannot book darshan, poojas or rooms, accept donations, or answer on behalf of Shri Shaneshwar Devasthan. For those, please use the trust's own website: [shanidev.com]({{temple.officialWebsite}}).
:::

## We'd love to hear from you about

- **Corrections** — if something on this site is wrong or out of date. Please include the page address and, if possible, a source.
- **Updates** — confirmed changes to timings, transport, facilities or festival arrangements.
- **Photographs** — original photos you are happy for us to publish with credit.
- **Suggestions** — topics you would like us to cover.
