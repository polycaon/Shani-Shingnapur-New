## Route overview

Head south-west on the Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar–Ahilyanagar highway. Turn off towards Shingnapur in the Ghodegaon area, then follow the district road to the village. Check the turn on a live map — it is easy to miss on the busy highway.

## Travel time by mode

| Mode | Typical one-way time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private car / taxi | About 1.75–2.5 hours | Highway traffic varies |
| MSRTC bus | Longer | May need a change near the turn-off or at Ahilyanagar |
| Flight + taxi | — | Airport (IXU) is on the city side; allow ~2 hours by road |

## Things to know

- The city's name changed from Aurangabad in 2023; many signs and bookings still use the old name.
- If you are visiting Ellora, remember that the caves are closed on Tuesdays.

## Nearby attractions

Ellora Caves, Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga, Bibi Ka Maqbara and Daulatabad Fort. See [Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar](/nearby-places/chhatrapati-sambhajinagar/).
