## How we arrive at these figures

Distances are shown as **ranges** because the road distance depends on the exact route, your starting point within a city, and any diversions. We compare several mapping and travel sources and round to sensible ranges rather than quoting a single, falsely precise number. Drive times assume a private vehicle, normal traffic and short stops only. Read [how we research](/how-we-research/) for more.
