## Route overview

Shingnapur lies north-east of Ahilyanagar city. The drive uses district roads towards Sonai and Shingnapur, passing through farmland and small villages. Traffic leaving the city can be slow at peak times.

## Getting there from Ahilyanagar

| Mode | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Taxi | Available from the railway station, bus stand and hotels; agree a round-trip price |
| Auto-rickshaw | Possible for the city leg; less comfortable for the full distance |
| MSRTC bus | Services from the city bus stand towards Sonai and Shingnapur; check timings locally |
| Own vehicle | Straightforward drive; use a live map for the final turns |

## Why Ahilyanagar makes a good base

- **Rail access:** Ahilyanagar station (ANG) has many more trains than Rahuri, the geographically closest station. See [by train](/travel/by-train/).
- **Bus hub:** Frequent MSRTC services to Pune, Shirdi, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and beyond.
- **Hotels and food:** A wide range at city prices. See [accommodation](/travel/accommodation/).

## A note on the name

The district and city were known as **Ahmednagar** until the official renaming to **Ahilyanagar** in October 2024. Railway, bus and map listings may use either name.
