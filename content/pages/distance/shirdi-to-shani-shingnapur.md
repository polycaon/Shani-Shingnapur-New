## Route overview

The usual route runs south from Shirdi through **Rahata** towards **Rahuri**, then turns east towards **Sonai** and on to Shingnapur. Map apps sometimes suggest an alternative via Shrirampur and Nevasa-side roads when traffic is heavy.

## Travel time by mode

| Mode | Typical one-way time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private car / taxi | About 1.5–2 hours | Longer on Saturdays and festivals |
| Shared jeep / cab | About 2–2.5 hours | Plus waiting time for the vehicle to fill |
| MSRTC bus | About 2–3 hours | Depends on route and connections |

These are typical ranges, not guarantees.

## Things to know

- **Round trips are standard.** Most visitors return to Shirdi the same day; allow 4–6 hours in total on a weekday.
- **Saturdays are much busier** at Shingnapur, and traffic on the route increases.
- **Taxi packages** usually include waiting time — confirm before you go. See [from Shirdi](/travel/from-shirdi/) for booking advice.

## Along the way and nearby

- **Rahuri** — a market town on the route.
- **Ahilyanagar** — about an hour beyond Shingnapur if you are continuing south. See [Ahilyanagar](/nearby-places/ahmednagar/).
