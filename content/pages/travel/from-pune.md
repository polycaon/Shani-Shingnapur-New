## Road routes

[[route-options id=pune]]

The route via **Ahilyanagar** is the usual choice: the highway is well used and there are plenty of places to stop for fuel and food. The route via **Alephata and Sangamner** makes more sense if you plan to visit Shirdi as well.

## By bus

MSRTC runs frequent buses between Pune and Ahilyanagar (from Pune's main bus stands), and you can continue by local bus or taxi. Direct services to Shingnapur may run on some days — check the [MSRTC website](https://msrtc.maharashtra.gov.in/) or ask at the bus stand.

## By train

Trains run from Pune to Ahilyanagar via Daund. From Ahilyanagar station, take a taxi or bus for the final hour. See [by train](/travel/by-train/).

## Planning a day trip from Pune

A day trip is very achievable:

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Early morning | Leave Pune |
| Mid-morning | Breakfast stop on the highway; pass Ahilyanagar |
| Late morning | Arrive at Shingnapur; darshan |
| Early afternoon | Lunch; optional short visit in Ahilyanagar |
| Evening | Return to Pune |

Timings shift with traffic and crowds; treat this as a sketch. For a longer trip, add Shirdi and stay overnight — see the [two-day itinerary](/pilgrimage/shirdi-shani-shingnapur-two-day-itinerary/).

## Worth a stop: Ahilyanagar

Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar) is right on your route. The historic **Ahmednagar Fort** and other sights make a pleasant break. See [Ahilyanagar](/nearby-places/ahmednagar/).
