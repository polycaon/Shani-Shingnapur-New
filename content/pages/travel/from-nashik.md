## Road routes

[[route-options id=nashik]]

The route through **Shirdi** is the natural choice for pilgrims, because it lets you combine Sai Baba darshan with Shani darshan in a single trip.

## By bus

Buses between Nashik and Shirdi are frequent. From Shirdi, continue by shared jeep, bus or taxi — see [from Shirdi](/travel/from-shirdi/).

## By train

There is no direct rail route that helps much here. Nashik Road station is on the Mumbai–Bhusawal line; reaching Ahilyanagar by train generally means changing at Manmad. For most travellers, road is simpler.

## Combining with Trimbakeshwar and Shirdi

Nashik is the gateway to one of Maharashtra's great pilgrimage circuits:

- **Trimbakeshwar** Jyotirlinga, about 28 km from Nashik — see [Trimbakeshwar](/nearby-places/trimbakeshwar/)
- **Shirdi**, on the way to Shingnapur — see [Shirdi](/nearby-places/shirdi/)
- **Shani Shingnapur**

Seeing all three comfortably takes two to three days. Our [Maharashtra temple tour](/pilgrimage/maharashtra-temple-tour/) suggests how to fit them together.
