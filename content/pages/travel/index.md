## Getting there

Shani Shingnapur is a village without its own railway station or airport. The simplest way to think about any trip is in two stages:

1. **Reach a hub** — Shirdi, Ahilyanagar, Pune, Nashik or Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar — by train, flight, bus or car.
2. **Cover the last stretch by road** — by taxi, hired car, MSRTC bus or shared jeep.
