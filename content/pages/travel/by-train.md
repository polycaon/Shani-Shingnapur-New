## Which station should you choose?

- **Ahilyanagar (ANG)** — the best-connected option. It sits on the Daund–Manmad line, with trains to Pune, Mumbai (via Pune/Daund), Nashik Road, and long-distance destinations across India. The city also has plenty of taxis, buses and hotels. From the station to the temple is roughly an hour by road.
- **Rahuri (RRI)** — geographically the closest, but only some trains stop. Useful if your train does stop there, but check onward transport in advance.
- **Belapur (BAP)**, serving Shrirampur — another stop on the Daund–Manmad line; handy if it fits your train.
- **Sainagar Shirdi (SNSI)** — ideal if you are visiting Shirdi first; Shingnapur is then a road trip of about 1.5–2 hours.

Station names and codes can change — the Ahmednagar district itself was renamed Ahilyanagar in 2024. Use the station code when searching timetables.

## Checking trains

Timetables and stops change. We do not list individual trains, because such lists go out of date quickly. Use:

- [IRCTC](https://www.irctc.co.in/) for bookings.
- [National Train Enquiry System](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/) for live running status and stops.

## From the station to the temple

- **Taxi or auto-rickshaw** — available outside Ahilyanagar and Shirdi stations; agree the fare first.
- **MSRTC bus** — from the city bus stand (not the railway station) at Ahilyanagar; check the route to Sonai or Shingnapur.
- **Hotel pickup** — some hotels arrange transfers; ask when booking.

## Suggested train-based plans

| Starting from | Suggested approach |
| --- | --- |
| Pune | Train to Ahilyanagar → taxi or bus to Shingnapur (or drive the whole way) |
| Mumbai | Train to Ahilyanagar or Sainagar Shirdi → road to Shingnapur |
| Nashik | Train to Ahilyanagar via Manmad, or road via Shirdi (usually simpler) |
| Other states | Train to Ahilyanagar, Shirdi, Manmad or Pune → road |
