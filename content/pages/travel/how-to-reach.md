## By train

There is no station at Shingnapur. The practical options are:

[[stations]]

For most travellers **Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar)** is the better choice because far more trains stop there. Taxis and buses connect the station with Shingnapur. See [Shani Shingnapur by train](/travel/by-train/).

## By air

[[airports]]

Shirdi Airport is the closest, but it has limited routes that change seasonally. Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and Pune airports have more regular connections. From any airport, continue by taxi. See [Shani Shingnapur by air](/travel/by-air/).

## By bus

The Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (**MSRTC**) runs buses across the region. Travellers commonly report:

- Services between **Ahilyanagar** and Shingnapur or nearby Sonai.
- Services and shared jeeps between **Shirdi** and Shingnapur, especially for pilgrims.
- Frequent long-distance buses to Ahilyanagar and Shirdi from Pune, Mumbai, Nashik and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

We have not verified current timetables. Check the [MSRTC website](https://msrtc.maharashtra.gov.in/) or ask at the bus stand. On busy days, direct buses can fill quickly.

## Taxi and cab

Taxis are the most convenient option for most visitors:

- **From Shirdi**, many operators offer Shirdi–Shingnapur round trips, and shared cabs run on busy days. See [from Shirdi](/travel/from-shirdi/).
- **From stations and airports**, prepaid or app-based cabs may be available at larger hubs; otherwise use local taxi stands.
- **For multi-day tours**, hiring a car with a driver lets you combine Shingnapur with Shirdi, Nashik, Trimbakeshwar or Ellora.

We do not list fares because they change and vary by vehicle and season. Before you set off, agree the route, number of stops, waiting time, tolls, parking and total price, and get it in writing (a message is fine).

## When you arrive

Vehicles are parked in areas along the temple approach; on crowded days you may be asked to park further away and walk. See [parking](/travel/parking/) and then the [darshan guide](/temple/darshan/).
