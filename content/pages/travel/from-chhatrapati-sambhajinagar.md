## About the city's name

The city of Aurangabad was officially renamed **Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar** in 2023. You will still see "Aurangabad" on older maps, tickets and signs, and the airport code remains IXU.

## By road

[[route-options id=chhatrapati-sambhajinagar]]

The highway is busy with trucks and buses, so allow extra time. Look carefully for the turn-off towards Shingnapur, and confirm it on a live map.

## By bus

MSRTC buses run frequently between Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and Ahilyanagar. You may be able to get off near the Shingnapur turn-off and continue by shared jeep or auto-rickshaw, or ride to Ahilyanagar and change. Check routes at the central bus stand or on the [MSRTC website](https://msrtc.maharashtra.gov.in/).

## Flying into Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

The airport (IXU) has regular domestic flights. From the airport, a taxi to Shingnapur takes around 2 hours. See [by air](/travel/by-air/).

## Combining with Ellora and Grishneshwar

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar is the base for the **Ellora Caves** (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) and **Grishneshwar**, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, which sits beside Ellora at Verul. A two-day plan might be:

- **Day 1:** Ellora and Grishneshwar; overnight in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.
- **Day 2:** Early drive to Shingnapur; darshan; continue to Shirdi or return.

Remember that the Ellora Caves are **closed on Tuesdays**. See [Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar](/nearby-places/chhatrapati-sambhajinagar/).
