## Staying in Shani Shingnapur

Staying in the village lets you take darshan early, before day-trippers arrive — especially valuable on Saturdays and festival days.

- **Trust accommodation.** The {{temple.trust}} operates devotee accommodation (*bhakta niwas*). Details, availability and booking arrangements should be checked on the [trust's website]({{temple.officialWebsite}}) or at the trust office.
- **Private lodges and guest houses.** Simple rooms are available in and around the village. Standards vary, so look at recent reviews and inspect the room if you can.

## Staying in Shirdi

Shirdi has one of the largest concentrations of pilgrim accommodation in Maharashtra, from simple lodges to large hotels. The Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust also operates accommodation for devotees — see [sai.org.in](https://sai.org.in/). Staying in Shirdi makes sense if you are visiting both temples; see [Shirdi](/nearby-places/shirdi/).

## Staying in Ahilyanagar

Ahilyanagar is a sizeable city with hotels in several price ranges, good rail and bus connections, and a quieter atmosphere than Shirdi. It suits travellers arriving from Pune or by train. See [Ahilyanagar](/nearby-places/ahmednagar/).

## Choosing and booking

- **Book early for weekends and festivals.** Saturdays, long weekends, Shani Jayanti and Shani Amavasya fill rooms quickly, particularly in the village.
- **Check what "near the temple" means.** Some listings stretch the definition.
- **Read recent reviews** for cleanliness, water supply and noise.
- **Confirm check-in times** if you arrive very early for darshan.
- **Ask about parking** if you are driving.
- **Beware of lookalike booking sites** claiming to be official temple portals.
