## Comparing your options

### Shirdi Airport (SAG), Kakadi

The closest airport. It mainly serves pilgrims to Shirdi, and is a good choice if you plan to visit both Shirdi and Shingnapur. Routes and schedules are limited and can change with the season, so check airline websites before planning around it.

### Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport (IXU)

Formerly called Aurangabad Airport. It has regular domestic flights and is convenient if you also want to see the **Ellora Caves** and **Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga**. The drive to Shingnapur is roughly 2 hours. See [from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar](/travel/from-chhatrapati-sambhajinagar/).

### Pune Airport (PNQ)

Usually the widest choice of domestic flights among the nearby airports. The drive is longer — about 3.5–4.5 hours — but the road via Ahilyanagar is straightforward. See [from Pune](/travel/from-pune/).

### Mumbai (BOM)

The main international gateway. From Mumbai, most visitors continue by road (6–8 hours), by train, or by a connecting flight to Shirdi, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar or Pune. See [from Mumbai](/travel/from-mumbai/).

## From the airport to the temple

- **Prepaid or app-based taxis** may be available at larger airports; otherwise use the official taxi stand.
- **Pre-booked cars** through your hotel or a tour operator are convenient for early or late flights.
- **Public transport** from airports to Shingnapur is limited; you would normally need to go into the city first.

Agree the full fare, tolls and waiting charges before starting.

## Tips for international visitors

- Mumbai is usually the most practical arrival point, with onward domestic flights or trains.
- Keep a printed copy of your hotel address and the temple's name — **Shri Shaneshwar Devasthan, Shani Shingnapur** — for drivers.
- Allow a rest day after long-haul flights before a long road trip.
