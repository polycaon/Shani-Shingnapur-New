## What we know — and what we don't

:::verify Information we are still confirming
We have not yet verified the current locations, capacity or charges of parking areas at Shingnapur. Arrangements are managed locally and can change, especially on festival days. The advice below is general. If you have up-to-date information, please [let us know](/contact/).
:::

Visitors consistently describe parking areas along the approach roads to the temple, from which you walk to the shrine, leaving footwear at a stand before entering. Parking attendants may direct vehicles and collect a fee; ask for a receipt.

## On ordinary days

- Parking near the temple approach is usually manageable on weekday mornings.
- Taxi drivers from Shirdi and Ahilyanagar know where to park and will usually wait for you.
- Note where you parked, or save the location on your phone, before you walk away.

## On Saturdays and festival days

On Saturdays, [Shani Amavasya](/festivals/shani-amavasya/) and [Shani Jayanti](/festivals/shani-jayanti/):

- Traffic can back up on the approach roads.
- Police and temple volunteers may set up diversions and stop vehicles well before the village.
- You may need to walk a considerable distance — plan for elders and children.
- Arriving very early is the single best way to find parking close in.

## Safety tips

- **Lock your vehicle** and keep valuables out of sight — the village's door-free tradition does not extend to parking lots.
- **Do not leave pets or children** in parked cars; the plateau gets very hot.
- **Agree with your driver** on where to meet and exchange phone numbers.
- **Follow attendants' instructions** and keep emergency lanes clear.

## If you are coming by taxi

Most taxi packages from Shirdi include parking and the driver's waiting time. Confirm this before you set off. See [from Shirdi](/travel/from-shirdi/).
