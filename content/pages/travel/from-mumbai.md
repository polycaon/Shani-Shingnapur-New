## Choosing a road route

| Route | Why choose it | Things to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Via Nashik and Shirdi | Lets you visit Shirdi (and even Nashik/Trimbakeshwar) on the way | Busy highway traffic around Nashik and Shirdi |
| Via Pune and Ahilyanagar | Uses the Mumbai–Pune Expressway; good if you are visiting Pune | Pune city traffic can add time |
| Via Malshej Ghat and Alephata | Scenic; often the most direct | Ghat section is slow in fog and heavy rain; check conditions in the monsoon |

Check a live map on the day: traffic and road works decide which route is fastest.

## By train

Trains from Mumbai reach **Ahilyanagar** (often via Pune/Daund) and **Sainagar Shirdi**. From Ahilyanagar the temple is about an hour away by road; from Shirdi, about 1.5–2 hours. Overnight trains let you arrive early and do darshan the same morning. See [by train](/travel/by-train/).

## By bus

MSRTC and private operators run buses from Mumbai to Shirdi and to Ahilyanagar, including overnight services. Continue by local bus, shared jeep or taxi. See the [MSRTC website](https://msrtc.maharashtra.gov.in/).

## By air

Flying is rarely faster than a train or drive for this distance once airport time is included, but connecting flights to **Shirdi**, **Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar** or **Pune** can make sense if you are arriving in Mumbai from abroad. See [by air](/travel/by-air/).

## One day or two?

A same-day round trip from Mumbai means 12–16 hours on the road plus darshan time. It is possible with a hired car and a very early start, but it is exhausting — particularly for elders and children.

We recommend:

- **Two days**, staying overnight in Shirdi, Shingnapur or Ahilyanagar. See the [two-day itinerary](/pilgrimage/shirdi-shani-shingnapur-two-day-itinerary/).
- **Three days** if you also want Nashik and Trimbakeshwar — see the [Maharashtra temple tour](/pilgrimage/maharashtra-temple-tour/).

## Tips for Mumbai travellers

- **Leave before dawn** to clear city traffic.
- **Avoid Friday evenings** for departure if you can — weekend traffic out of Mumbai is heavy.
- **Book accommodation early** for weekends and festival dates. See [accommodation](/travel/accommodation/).
- **In the monsoon**, favour the expressway or highway routes over the Malshej Ghat.
