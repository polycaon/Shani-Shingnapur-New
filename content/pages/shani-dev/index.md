## Who is Shani Dev?

**Shani Dev** — also called Shanaishchara, Shaneshwar, Shani Maharaj or simply Shani — is one of the most widely worshipped deities in India, and one of the most misunderstood. He is the deity associated with the planet **Saturn**, and one of the nine *navagraha*, the planetary deities of Hindu tradition.

His names say something about how he is imagined. *Shanaishchara* is usually explained as "the slow-moving one" — a fitting name for Saturn, the slowest of the planets visible to the naked eye, which takes about 29.5 years to circle the zodiac.

## Why he is worshipped

In popular belief, Shani Dev is the **dispenser of karma**: he gives each person the fruits of their own actions, good and bad. That makes him feared — people often associate him with delays, hardship and tests of patience — but also deeply respected. Devotees see him as strict but fair, a teacher of discipline, humility and perseverance, and a protector of those who live honestly.

People turn to Shani Dev especially during periods that astrologers link with Saturn, such as **Sade Sati**. The pages below explain these ideas as they are understood in tradition, without making promises about outcomes.

## Shani Dev and Shingnapur

Among India's many Shani shrines, [Shani Shingnapur](/temple/) is unusual for its open-air form: the deity is worshipped as a self-manifested stone under the open sky.
