## Verification labels

Where information can change or is uncertain, we label it:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sourced** | Corroborated by the public sources listed on the page |
| **Approximate** | A range or estimate — for example a road distance — where exact values vary |
| **Unverified** | Widely reported, but not yet confirmed with the temple trust or an official source |
| **Awaiting verification** | We do not yet have reliable information, so we do not give a value |

## Temple information

For timings, rituals and facilities, the **temple trust** is the authoritative source. Where the trust's published information is unavailable to us or unclear, we describe what is commonly reported and label it **unverified** — rather than picking one version and presenting it as fact. When third-party sources disagree, we say so.

## Historical information

We distinguish carefully between:

- **Dated events** reported by reputable newspapers, courts or official publications, and
- **Traditional stories**, which we present as tradition.

We avoid popular but unsupported claims such as precise ages for traditions, unless a reliable source supports them.

## Distances and travel times

Road distances vary with the route and starting point. We compare several mapping and travel sources and publish **ranges**, rounded sensibly. Travel times assume a private vehicle, normal traffic and short stops. We do not publish fares, bus timetables or train lists, which change too often to keep accurate.

## Festival dates

Lunar festival dates change every year. Our [festival calendar](/festivals/festival-calendar/) **calculates** Amavasya, Shani Amavasya and Shani Jayanti dates using standard astronomical algorithms for the positions of the Sun and Moon, with Indian Standard Time and a sunrise-based convention. These are labelled as calculated. When an authoritative published date is available, it replaces the calculation and is marked as sourced.

## Religious content

We describe beliefs and practices as they are held by devotees, using sources such as widely used devotional texts and reputable reference works. We do not endorse astrological predictions or make promises about the effects of worship.

## Sources we use

Pages list their key references under **Sources & references**. Typical sources include:

- Shri Shaneshwar Devasthan (the temple trust)
- District Administration, Ahilyanagar
- Maharashtra Tourism and MTDC
- Indian Railways, IRCTC and the National Train Enquiry System
- Airports Authority of India
- MSRTC
- UNESCO (for World Heritage Sites)
- Established national newspapers for dated events
