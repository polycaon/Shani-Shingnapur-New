## Why visit

Shirdi is the town where **Sai Baba of Shirdi**, the revered saint who died in 1918, lived for most of his life. His followers include people of many faiths, and Shirdi is among the most visited pilgrimage towns in India. The shrine and its related sites are managed by the **Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust**.

## Things to see

- **Samadhi Mandir** — the main shrine, where Sai Baba's mortal remains are interred.
- **Dwarkamai** — the old mosque where Sai Baba lived; the sacred fire (*dhuni*) he kept is maintained there.
- **Chavadi** — where Sai Baba slept on alternate nights; associated with a well-known procession.
- **Gurusthan** — the site under a neem tree where Sai Baba is said to have first been seen in Shirdi.
- **Lendi Baug** — a garden Sai Baba is said to have tended.
- **Museum** — displaying objects associated with Sai Baba.

For current darshan arrangements, aarti timings and any pass system, check the trust's website at [sai.org.in](https://sai.org.in/).

## Pilgrimage relevance

Shirdi and Shingnapur are frequently visited together, and many pilgrims regard a visit to one as incomplete without the other. See [planning a combined pilgrimage](/pilgrimage/shirdi-shani-shingnapur/).

## Suggested duration

- **Minimum:** half a day for darshan and the main sites.
- **Comfortable:** a full day, with an overnight stay.
- **With Shingnapur:** one long day or, better, two days.

## Things to know

- **Thursdays** are Sai Baba's special day and are busier; weekends and holidays are busy too.
- **Major festivals** — including Ram Navami, Guru Purnima and Vijayadashami (the anniversary of Sai Baba's *samadhi*) — draw enormous crowds.
- **Accommodation** is plentiful. See [accommodation](/travel/accommodation/).
- **Transport:** Sainagar Shirdi railway station and Shirdi Airport serve the town.

## How it connects with Shani Shingnapur

Most pilgrims stay in Shirdi and make a taxi round trip to Shingnapur. See [Shirdi to Shani Shingnapur](/travel/from-shirdi/).
