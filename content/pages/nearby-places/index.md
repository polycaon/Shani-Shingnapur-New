## Closer to home: Nevasa

Shingnapur lies in **Nevasa** taluka. The town of Nevasa is associated in Marathi tradition with the saint-poet **Sant Dnyaneshwar**, who is traditionally said to have composed the *Dnyaneshwari*, his celebrated Marathi commentary on the Bhagavad Gita, there in the late thirteenth century. Devotees of the Varkari tradition visit the site associated with that event. If you are interested, ask locally for directions and current visiting arrangements.
