## Why visit

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar — renamed from Aurangabad in 2023 — is the gateway to some of India's most remarkable religious monuments. For a pilgrim, the highlights are:

- **Grishneshwar**, one of the twelve **Jyotirlinga** temples of Shiva, at Verul.
- The **Ellora Caves**, right next door — a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** with rock-cut Buddhist, Hindu and Jain monuments. The best known is the **Kailasa temple (Cave 16)**, carved from a single mass of rock.

## Pilgrimage relevance

Combining Grishneshwar with Trimbakeshwar lets you visit two Jyotirlingas in one trip, with Shirdi and Shani Shingnapur in between. See the [Maharashtra temple tour](/pilgrimage/maharashtra-temple-tour/).

## Suggested duration

- **One day:** Grishneshwar + Ellora.
- **Two days:** add Daulatabad and city sights.
- **Three days:** add Ajanta.

## Things to know

- **Weekly closures:** Ellora is closed on Tuesdays; Ajanta on Mondays. Check the Archaeological Survey of India's current notices.
- **Temple dress rules** at Grishneshwar for entry to the inner sanctum have been reported; check current guidance locally.
- **Heat:** Ellora involves a lot of walking in the open — go early in summer.

## How it connects with Shani Shingnapur

Drive along the Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar–Ahilyanagar highway and turn off near Ghodegaon. See [from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar](/travel/from-chhatrapati-sambhajinagar/).
